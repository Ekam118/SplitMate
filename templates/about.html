Welcome to SplitMate

A simple way to manage shared expenses, track payments, and settle balances with friends, family, and teams.

Create Groups

Organize trips, events, roommates, and shared activities in one place.

Manage Expenses

Add expenses and track who paid and who participated.

Track Balances

Easily see pending amounts and complete settlements.

How to Use SplitMate

1. Create a Group: Create a group for your trip, friends, family, or team.

2. Add Expenses: Record expenses with amount, payer, and participants.

3. Check Balances: SplitMate automatically calculates everyone's share.

4. Settle Payments: Complete payments and keep your records updated.
